### PROOF naming

I use **PROOF Fog & Friction Finder**, as Erik selected on 14 September. Finder describes the work without overstating what the answers establish. The older Forensics descriptor is retired in new copy and revised artwork.

**PROOF Trace replaces AI Infrastructure Assessment.** I agree with the change: Trace names the action more clearly. It remains one scoped assessment, one case and one intake. BizBlueprint converts the finding into operating design; BBAI implements and checks the agreed changes.

The existing line stays:

> See where people are carrying what the system can't.

**Ambiguity Doesn't Automate.** remains supporting editorial copy.

### Is Momentum Map a broad summary of PROOF?

**Yes, as the first business-level view of the same case. Its meaning depends on the evidence available.**

Before a trace, it summarizes the answer pattern and identifies something worth investigating. After a trace, it can summarize the observed findings, with the traced tasks and coverage made clear. It never implies that a small sample represents every part of the business.

I would not put a separate, repeated “PROOF quiz” behind the Momentum Map. I would not imply a complete measured report already exists behind an email field. The first Map should stand on its own: pattern, supporting answers, likely human repair, and one investigation.

## 2. Messaging hierarchy

### The shared message

**Purpose:** Freedom to live and work to good purpose.

**Problem:** AI agents and tools can change the quantity, speed, and flow of work faster than the surrounding setup adapts. People absorb the searching, chasing, checking, and rebuilding between steps.

**Condition:** Digital Fog names that gap work. Digital Friction describes the drag and stalled work it can produce.

**Route:** Recognize the pattern. Locate the gap. Take the next action that fits. Check what changes.

**Canonical action line:**

> Map the gap. Align the Infra. Build Fog-Free.

This is the current master wording. “Free the Fog” is an older version.
